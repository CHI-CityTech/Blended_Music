# Blended Music

## Project Overview

Blended Music is an open, evolving platform dedicated to the creative, technical, and conceptual integration of musical systems, traditions, and technologies. Its core mission is to explore how music can serve as both a medium and a laboratory for meaningful blending—across genres, cultures, disciplines, and emerging technologies.


## What Makes Blended Music Unique?

- **Integration, not fusion:** The project investigates how distinct musical and conceptual systems can interact, co-exist, and generate new expressive forms—while respecting the integrity of each source.
- **Breadth of scope:** Projects range from cross-cultural composition and algorithmic music to AI-human collaboration, quantum music, virtual orchestras, new instrument/interface design, and cross-modal integration (e.g., sonification, mapping data into musical structures, and the investigation of formalism and inspiration).
- **Open to all disciplines:** While music is the central medium, any field can participate—using music to communicate, research, or create in ways that reflect their own discipline’s perspective.
- **Emphasis on process and documentation:** Each project documents its research, design, and creative process, supporting reproducibility and future extension.

## Goals

- Advance understanding of how musical, cultural, and technological systems can be blended to create new forms of expression and research.
- Support projects that are experimental, analytical, educational, or performative.
- Provide a home for work that does not fit neatly into a single genre, tradition, or technology.
- Encourage respectful, well-documented integration—avoiding superficial “fusion” in favor of deep structural and conceptual engagement.

## What You’ll Find Here

- **Project Snapshots:** Examples include:
	- Gamelan–Yoruba composition for Blended Shadow Puppet Theatre
	- Quantum Musico & Meta-Mozart (AI, ethics, and recursive authorship)
	- Japanese–Western hybrid composition and sound design
	- UNESCO tradition integration and world-building
- **Research and Documentation:** Analytical papers, technical notes, and process documentation.
- **Tools and Assets:** Code, scripts, DAW templates, virtual instruments, and media assets for creative and technical work.

## How Blended Music Relates to Other Work

Blended Music often collaborates with or supports related initiatives (e.g., Blended Shadow Puppet, Balanced Blended Space, UNESCO projects, Collaborative AI, fictive world-building), but its primary focus is on the unique challenges and opportunities of musical integration.

## Who Should Get Involved?

Anyone interested in the creative, technical, or conceptual blending of music—composers, technologists, researchers, educators, and those from any discipline who see music as a bridge to new ideas.
